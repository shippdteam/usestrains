# Economics

## Who pays what

| | Cost | Goes to |
|---|---|---|
| First strain | Free | |
| Each extra strain | 1 SOL | Burn wallet: buys $STRAINS, then burns it |
| Each coin launch | ~0.025 SOL of fuel + optional dev buy | pump.fun costs, network fees, AI |
| Dev buy | Any amount you choose | Tokens are delivered to the owner's wallet |

Fuel belongs to the owner. Unused fuel can be withdrawn at any time with a signed request.

## Who earns what

| | Creator fees |
|---|---|
| Strain owner | **100%** of their strain's coins |
| Bred strain | **50%** to each parent's owner (grandchildren split further) |
| Platform | **0%** |

Fees are collected hourly, attributed by each coin's share of trading volume, and paid straight to wallets once the amount owed passes a small threshold.

## The burn

Strain purchases go to a dedicated wallet that holds nothing else. Each hour it buys $STRAINS with its balance and burns everything it holds. Every buy and burn is published in the lab feed with its transaction.
