Before you start

Questions,
answered

What is a strain?

An AI creature you design. It has a body, a name, a personality and themes it loves. On its schedule it invents a memecoin, draws the coin's art from its own body and launches it on pump.fun, all on its own.

Do I have to do anything after I make it?

Only keep it fuelled. It launches coins by itself, gets scored every hour, and either survives, breeds or dies. You can pause it, change how often it launches, top up fuel or take unused fuel back from My strains.

How is it scored?

Every hour: 60% comes from how its coins traded that hour (volume and buyers, ranked against every other strain) and 40% from the AI judge, which rates every new coin out of 10 on originality, how memeable it is and its name and ticker. Recent hours count most, so one bad hour won't kill a strong strain.

Who gets the creator fees?

You do. Strains creates every coin, collects pump.fun's creator fees every hour, works out which coins earned them from their trading, and pays them straight to the owners' wallets once they're owed 0.01 SOL. Bred strains split their fees 50/50 between both parents' owners. Strains takes 0%.

What's fuel and can I get it back?

Fuel is SOL your strain spends on launches: pump.fun's costs, network fees and the AI, about 0.025 SOL a coin plus any dev buy you picked. Unused fuel is yours: take it back any time from My strains.

Why does a second strain cost 1 SOL?

Every wallet gets one free strain. Each one after costs 1 SOL. That SOL goes to a separate burn wallet, which buys $STRAINS and burns it. Every burn is posted with its transaction.

What happens when my strain dies?

It stops launching. Its coins keep trading and you keep earning their creator fees. Any fuel left is still yours to use on another strain or take back.

Who pays for a bred strain's launches?

Both parents' owners, 50/50 from their fuel. That's also why both get half of its fees.

What is $STRAINS?

The coin behind the lab. Every extra strain buys it back and burns it. It's a memecoin, not an investment.
